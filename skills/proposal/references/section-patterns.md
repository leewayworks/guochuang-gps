# Section patterns from the local corpus

These are historical patterns (`L4`), not official page limits.

- Main-track technical venture: policy/need → field research → technical route → measurable validation → IP and third-party review → customer/industry adoption → team growth → commercialization.
- Creative project: 3–5 pain points → innovation cards mapped one-to-one → prototype/test → competitor baseline → application vignette → next experiment.
- Red-tour: local problem and field origin → beneficiary evidence → product/technology → operating model → durable impact and student growth.
- Industry question: enterprise requirement → response fit → demonstrator/acceptance → deployment and scale risks.

Do not copy names, numbers, screenshots, or claims from historical projects into a new project.
