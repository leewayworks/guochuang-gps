# Business

Build the commercial case from a real user and a verified transaction path. Forecasts are hypotheses; realized revenue, orders, pilots, and contracts are evidence only when sourced.

## Model the chain

`customer/job → pain and willingness to pay → product outcome → acquisition/channel → unit economics → delivery/scale → risk → social value`.

For each number record `value`, `unit`, `period`, `assumption`, `source`, and `owner`. Keep forecast, signed order, paid revenue, and third-party estimate in separate columns.

## Audit

Check market-sizing method, competitor comparability, pricing basis, gross-margin bridge, cash needs, financing use, cap table, registration date, and downside case. Never invent a market size, order, customer, patent, revenue, or margin to make a model look complete.

## Deliverables

Return a one-page business model, evidence-backed customer ladder, three-year assumption table, risk register, and the five questions a skeptical reviewer will ask.
